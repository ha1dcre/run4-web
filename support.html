Support

Questions, bug reports, and feedback for RUN4 are welcome in English.

What to include

  • RUN4 app version and build number
  • iPhone or iPad model and iOS version
  • the steps that reproduce the problem
  • the AUv3 host or external MIDI/Link setup, if relevant

RUN4 can prepare a local diagnostics export under Settings > General > Diagnostics. Review it before attaching it. Please do not send projects or audio recordings unless they are necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.

Self-help

The app contains a searchable manual under Settings > Help, including recording, Tape, Poly, Disc, Grain, Tone, Color, Air, MIDI, Ableton Link, AUv3, export, and troubleshooting.
